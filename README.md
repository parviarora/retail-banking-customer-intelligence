# Retail Banking Customer Intelligence & Campaign Optimization

## Overview

An end-to-end retail banking analytics project focused on customer intelligence, segmentation, campaign optimization, and profitability-driven decision making.

The project examines how a retail bank can use Customer 360 data to identify high-value customers, understand customer behaviour, predict campaign response, and allocate marketing resources toward the highest-value opportunities.

## Business Objective

Develop an analytical framework to answer:

- Which customers represent the highest economic value?
- How can customers be segmented based on value and behaviour?
- Which products and channels generate the strongest campaign economics?
- Which customers are most likely to respond to a Personal Loan campaign?
- How should the bank allocate its campaign budget?

## Analytical Framework

Customer Data
→ Customer 360
→ Customer Segmentation
→ Campaign Analytics
→ Propensity Modelling
→ Campaign Economics
→ Strategic Decisioning

## Current Analysis

### Customer 360

Integrated customer demographics, account relationships, cards, loans, transactions and campaign activity into a customer-level analytical view using MySQL.

### Customer Segmentation

Developed a customer value scoring framework incorporating:

- Deposit balances
- Card spending
- Loan exposure
- Transaction activity
- Campaign responsiveness
- Credit risk indicators

### Campaign Analytics

Analysed campaign response, revenue, cost, contribution and ROI across products and channels.

Initial analysis indicates significant differences in campaign economics across channels, with lower-cost digital channels demonstrating stronger economics for several products.

### Predictive Analytics

Developing a logistic regression propensity model to estimate Personal Loan campaign response using customer financial, product, transaction and engagement characteristics.
